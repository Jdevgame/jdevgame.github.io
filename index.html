mailhub

A personal, self-hosted, read-only Gmail mirror.

mailhub is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer at home. It keeps a read-only copy of the owner's own Gmail accounts so they can search them locally, including through an AI assistant on their home network.

  • Read-only: it only requests the gmail.readonly permission. It cannot send, delete, label or change any email.
  • Private: it is not a public service. Only the owner connects their own accounts, and mail is stored only on the owner's device.
  • No third parties: no data is sold, shared or sent to any server operated by the developer.
